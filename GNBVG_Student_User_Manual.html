2 Step-by-Step Login Procedure

All 916 approved hostel residents are pre-registered on the official hostel database. Follow these steps to sign in:

1
Select "Resident Student" Mode
On the portal homepage, make sure the Resident Student tab is selected (active by default).
2
Enter Roll Number or College Email
Type your official GNITS Roll Number (e.g., 23251A0501) or your registered college email (e.g., 23251a0501@gnits.ac.in).
3
Enter Password
Enter your password. If you are logging in for the first time or forgot your password, refer to Section 4: Password Reset below.
4
Access Personal Resident Dashboard
Click "Sign In to Student Portal". Upon successful verification, you will see your Wing Block (Anapurna-A, Arundhathi-B, or Anuradha-C), your Room Number, and your Smart ID status.

3 Face Recognition (FRS) Enrollment & 3-Chance Re-capture Workflow

Every resident must complete one-time Facial Biometric Enrollment so the AI camera can record your attendance accurately each evening.

⚠
Pre-requisites for Face Capture: Ensure you are in a well-lit area. Remove sunglasses, caps, or heavy scarves. Look straight into the camera lens with a neutral expression.
1
Launch Camera in "Biometric Face ID Enrollment"
On your student dashboard, locate the Biometric Face ID Enrollment card and click "Open Camera & Start Enrollment".
2
Align Face in Target Oval & Capture
Fit your face inside the green circular target and click "Capture Photo". The AI instantly extracts a 128-dimensional facial biometric descriptor.
3
Review Image & Use Re-capture (Max 3 Chances)
A preview of your captured photo will be shown. If the photo is blurred or lighting was poor, you can click "Retake Photo". You are granted up to 3 total attempts to capture your best picture.
4
Confirm & Finalize Enrollment
Once satisfied, click "Save & Finalize Enrollment". The system saves your photo and biometric descriptor to the database.

What Happens After Successful Enrollment?

  • Automatic UI Update: The capture camera is hidden and replaced by a verified badge: ✓ Smart ID Card and FRS are successfully enrolled.
  • Digital Smart ID Card Generated: Your verified face photo and unique hostel barcode are embedded directly onto your Digital Smart ID Card.
  • Warden System Sync: Your status in the Warden Portal automatically updates from Pending to Enrolled.
🚨
Mistaken Face Submission? (Security Policy):
To prevent student tampering or proxy fraud, students CANNOT delete or reset their face profile themselves once finalized. If you accidentally submitted another resident's face or need a re-enrollment, you must meet your Hostel Wing Warden. Only the Warden possesses the administrative key to delete the face profile and unlock fresh enrollment chances.

4 How to Reset Your Password via 6-Digit OTP

If you forgot your password or need to update it, you can reset it instantly using the automated OTP verification:

1
Click "Forgot Password?"
On the Student Login box, click the orange "Forgot Password?" link located directly beneath the password field.
2
Enter Registered Roll Number or Email
Type your Roll Number (e.g., 23251A0501) and click "Send Verification OTP".
3
Enter the 6-Digit OTP
Check your email or screen notification for the secure 6-digit one-time code (e.g., 849201) and enter it into the OTP box.
4
Enter & Confirm New Password
Type your new password (minimum 6 characters) and click "Update Password". You can now immediately log in with your new credentials.

5 Daily Evening Attendance Routine & Geofencing Rules

Daily biometric roll call is mandatory for all hostel residents residing on campus.

Requirement Official Standard Notes / Action Required
Daily Timing Window 05:00 PM – 07:00 PM IST (17:00 – 19:00) Attendance outside this window is locked unless Warden opens an override.
Geofence Perimeter Within 100 meters of GNBVG Hostel Wings GPS check verifies you are physically present inside hostel courtyard.
Verification Method Face Biometric Match (AI Distance < 0.50) Instant scan compares live camera feed against your enrolled face.
Attendance Status Marked Present with precise timestamp Instantly reflected on Warden's live attendance dashboard.
⚠
Out-of-Range Geofence Breaches: If a resident attempts to mark attendance while outside the hostel perimeter, the system logs a high-priority Out-of-Range Breach Alert with your exact GPS coordinates directly to the Warden Portal for disciplinary review.

6 Smart ID Card, Outpasses & Maintenance Helpdesk

🆔
Digital Smart ID Card
Click "View Smart ID Card" from your portal to view your official photo ID with your Blood Group, Room Number, Parent Contact, and Warden Signature. You can present this on your phone screen during hostel entry or outing check-ins.
✈
Applying for Hostel Outpasses
Need to leave campus for vacation, weekend outing, or emergency? Go to "Outpass Requests", select Outing / Home Visit, specify dates, and submit. Your Wing Warden will review and approve it digitally.

Room Maintenance Tickets
Facing electrical, plumbing, carpentry, or Wi-Fi issues in your room? Post a ticket under "Maintenance Desk". The hostel facility team will be dispatched directly to your room.
