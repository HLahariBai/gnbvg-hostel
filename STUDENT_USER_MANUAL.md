# G. Narayanamma Building for Vocational Guidance (GNBVG)
## Resident Student Portal & Face Recognition System (FRS) User Manual

Welcome to the **GNBVG Hostel Digital Safety & Attendance Portal**. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for hostel residents on how to access the portal, activate your account, complete Face Biometric Enrollment, generate your Smart ID Card, mark daily attendance, and reset your password.

---

## 📌 Portal Access Links

- **Hostel Portal URL:** [https://partial-modules-populations-homeland.trycloudflare.com/hostel](https://partial-modules-populations-homeland.trycloudflare.com/hostel)
- **Local Campus Network:** `http://localhost:5000/hostel`
- **Supported Devices:** Any Smartphone (Android / iOS) or Laptop/PC equipped with a front camera.
- **Recommended Browser:** Google Chrome, Safari, or Microsoft Edge.

---

## 1. First-Time Registration & Account Activation

Every resident student is pre-listed in the official hostel admission database. You only need to activate your account once using your college Roll Number.

### Step 1: Open Portal
1. Navigate to the portal link above.
2. Ensure you are on the **Student Portal** tab (default view).

### Step 2: Request Activation OTP
1. Under **"New Resident Activation / Sign Up"**, enter your college **Roll Number** (e.g., `2301A0501`).
2. Click **"Verify Roster & Send OTP"**.
3. The portal will automatically verify your identity from the hostel master list and display your registered name, hostel wing/block, and room number.

### Step 3: Verify OTP & Create Password
1. Check your student email inbox for the **6-digit Verification Code**. *(If in test mode, the code will appear on screen).*
2. Enter the **6-Digit OTP**.
3. Choose a secure **Password** (minimum 6 characters).
4. Click **"Activate Account & Login"**.

---

## 2. Returning Student Login

Once your account is activated:
1. Open the portal and scroll to the **"Resident Sign In"** form.
2. Enter your **Roll Number** (or Registered Email) and your **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In to Resident Dashboard"**.

## 3. Face Recognition System (FRS) & Smart ID Card Enrollment

Your facial biometric data is required for hostel security, automated evening roll call, and to issue your official **Digital Smart ID Card**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Guidelines for Good Face Capture:**
> - Ensure your face is well-lit (avoid strong backlighting or shadows).
> - Remove sunglasses, caps, or face masks.
> - Look directly into the front camera.

### Step-by-Step Face Enrollment:

1. **Access Face Enrollment:**
   - Once logged in, locate the **"Face Biometrics & Smart ID Enrollment"** section on your dashboard.
2. **Open Camera:**
   - Click the green **"Open Camera / Start Sensor"** button.
   - When prompted by your phone or browser, tap **"Allow"** to grant camera access.
3. **Capture Photo:**
   - Position your face inside the oval scanning frame.
   - Click **"Capture Face Photo"**.
4. **Preview & Retake (Up to 3 Attempts):**
   - You will see a live preview of your captured photo.
   - If the photo is blurred or not centered, click **"Retake Photo"**.
   - You have up to **3 chances** to retake your photo before finalizing.
5. **Finalize Enrollment:**
   - Once satisfied with your photo, click **"Confirm & Finalize Enrollment"**.
   - Your facial embedding and photo are encrypted and saved securely to the hostel database.
6. **Smart ID Card Generation:**
   - A confirmation banner will state: **"Smart ID Card and FRS are successfully enrolled."**
   - The enrollment camera will automatically close, and your status will update to **`FRS Enrolled ✓`**.
   - Click **"View Smart ID Card"** to inspect or download your digital hostel ID card.

> [!WARNING]
> **Mistaken Face Capture:**
> If a resident mistakenly enrolls using someone else's face, the student cannot reset it themselves for security reasons. Please contact your **Hostel Block Warden**. The Warden has an administrative **"Reset Face"** button to clear the mistaken biometric profile and grant 3 fresh capture attempts.

## 4. How to Mark Daily Evening Attendance (FRS Roll Call)

- **Daily Attendance Window:** **05:00 PM to 07:00 PM IST** (17:00 – 19:00).
- **Location Requirement:** You must be physically present inside the **GNBVG Hostel Campus / Courtyard** (GPS Geofence verified).

### Steps to Mark Attendance:
1. Enable **Location / GPS** on your phone.
2. Open the portal between 5:00 PM and 7:00 PM IST.
3. In your dashboard, click **"Mark Evening Attendance"**.
4. Allow Camera and Location access.
5. Look at the camera for 1 second.
6. The system instantly verifies your face match against your enrolled profile and validates your hostel coordinates.
7. A green **"Attendance Marked: Present"** confirmation card will appear with your check-in timestamp.

## 5. How to Reset Your Password (Forgot Password)

If you forget your password, you can reset it in seconds using your registered email:

### Step 1: Open Password Reset Form
1. On the portal login page, look under the Resident Sign In box.
2. Click **"Forgot your password? Click here to reset"**.

### Step 2: Request Reset Code
1. Enter your college **Roll Number** or registered **Email Address**.
2. Click **"Send Verification Code"**.

### Step 3: Enter OTP & New Password
1. Check your email for the **6-digit Password Reset OTP**.
2. Enter the OTP code in the **"6-Digit OTP"** field.
3. Enter your **New Password**.
4. Confirm your new password and click **"Reset Password & Sign In"**.
5. Your password is now updated, and you can log in immediately.
